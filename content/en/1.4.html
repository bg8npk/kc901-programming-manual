1.4 Communication Example

To obtain the system time, send the following command:

$[command],[option]\n
$date,get\n

The complete exchange is shown below. Purple indicates host transmissions; blue indicates instrument responses.

$date,get\n
$start,date\n
Date:2025-12-30 15:22:14  ; Year, month, day, hour, minute, second, in that order.
$end\n
