3.2.12 S11 User Calibration Standard Parameters (Fourth-Generation KC901X; unavailable in firmware version 1.8.0 and later)

KC901K/RFMA uses the complex calibration model:

Command

Option

Description

userkit_s11

female

male

Enter the short and open standard parameters, in that order.

The short standard parameters are, in order:

L0,L1,L2,L3, one-way electrical length (mm), total loss (GΩ/m)

The open standard parameters are, in order:

C0,C1,C2,C3, one-way electrical length (mm), total loss (GΩ/m)

Range:

L0-L3 and C0-C3 range: -9999.999 to +9999.999

One-way electrical length: 0-999.999

Total loss: 0-9999.999

These are the calibration standard settings for user calibration, corresponding to the user calibration standard settings on the instrument's main Settings screen.

Description:

Use female for female calibration standards and male for male calibration standards.

Example:

Set the electrical lengths of the short and open standards to 5.25mm and 5.26mm, respectively. The load standard has no electrical length setting.

// L0,L1 , L2,L3,mm,GΩ/m C0, C1, C2, C3, mm, GΩ/m

$userkit_s11,female,10,125,-20,15,5.25,0.201,100,125,-800,15,5.26,0.200\n
