3.4.13 Frequency List Exceeds 10000 Points (Fourth-Generation KC901X)

The confirmed frequency-list limit is 10000 points. The following source response example still says 10001; its exact firmware text and the handling of an oversized list have not yet been confirmed. Do not infer a 10001-point limit from this unverified example.

$start,err_freq\n
$error: Over 10001 points!\n
$end\n
