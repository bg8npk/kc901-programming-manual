2.5 Disconnecting from the KC901

There are two ways to disconnect:

1. Send a disconnect command from the host. The source refers to "3.2.16 Exiting Remote-Control Mode"; the corresponding section in this edition is 3.2.19.

2. Press [SHIFT]+[MODE] on the KC901 to disconnect locally.

If the USB cable is accidentally unplugged or communication is interrupted for another reason, the KC901 does not detect the interruption automatically. Press [SHIFT]+[MODE] to terminate remote control. On fourth-generation KC901 instruments, use [SHIFT]+[HOME] instead.
